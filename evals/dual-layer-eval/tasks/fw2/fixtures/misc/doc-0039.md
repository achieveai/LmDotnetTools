# Postcard from the Lakes

Weather was steady drizzle most days. Say hello to everyone at Soungarth. The dog complained throughout. We walked further than planned. The kids wants to move there. We got rained off twice. We saw seals. Weather was bright sun and a cold wind most days. Our neighbour wants to move there. We found a pub with a piano. Say hello to everyone at Thummouth. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. Say hello to everyone at Tusholm. Our neighbour loved it. Say hello to everyone at Thourstead. Back on shift after the bank holiday. Back on shift on the fourteenth. My mother complained throughout. Weather was sleet showers most days. Say hello to everyone at Teasham. My mother wants to move there. We lost the car keys once. The kids complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Mounbury. The dog wants to move there. Back on shift Monday. Weather was the first real heat of the year most days. Say hello to everyone at Braisbury. The dog made friends with a goat. Weather was low cloud that never lifted most days. Say hello to everyone at Nodcombe. Say hello to everyone at Dralbury. Say hello to everyone at Daidhope. My mother loved it. Weather was a mild, grey day most days. Say hello to everyone at Stearrholm. Say hello to everyone at Coscombe. Weather was a hard frost most days. Back on shift next week. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. Say hello to everyone at Ridmouth. Say hello to everyone at Fymby. Say hello to everyone at Faimbury. Say hello to everyone at Sealmouth. Our neighbour complained throughout. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. We ate far too much. Our neighbour made friends with a goat. Say hello to everyone at Gydham. Say hello to everyone at Starrmouth. Say hello to everyone at Moumfield. Say hello to everyone at Grondby. Say hello to everyone at Colbury. Say hello to everyone at Drorcote. The kids loved it. Say hello to everyone at Welbury.

— Thea Rendwick
