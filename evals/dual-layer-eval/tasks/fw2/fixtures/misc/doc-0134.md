# Postcard from a canal boat on the Llangollen

We lost the car keys once. Say hello to everyone at Thourstead. We saw seals. Back on shift next week. Say hello to everyone at Nodcombe. Say hello to everyone at Grondby. The dog complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Starrmouth. Our neighbour complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Dralbury. Back on shift after the bank holiday. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. Say hello to everyone at Daidhope. Back on shift Monday. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. The kids made friends with a goat. Our neighbour wants to move there. Weather was bright sun and a cold wind most days. Back on shift on the fourteenth. Say hello to everyone at Stearrholm. The dog loved it. My mother wants to move there. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. Say hello to everyone at Drorcote. My mother made friends with a goat. My mother loved it. Weather was low cloud that never lifted most days. Say hello to everyone at Braisbury. The kids complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Gydham. My mother complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Tiscote. We walked further than planned. We got rained off twice. Weather was steady drizzle most days. The kids wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Tusholm. We ate far too much. The dog made friends with a goat. Weather was sleet showers most days. Weather was the first real heat of the year most days. Weather was a hard frost most days. Weather was a mild, grey day most days. The kids loved it. Our neighbour loved it. Say hello to everyone at Coscombe. Our neighbour made friends with a goat. We found a pub with a piano. Say hello to everyone at Thyrrholm. Say hello to everyone at Welbury. Say hello to everyone at Thummouth. Say hello to everyone at Colbury. Say hello to everyone at Fymby. Say hello to everyone at Mounbury. Say hello to everyone at Taidhope. Say hello to everyone at Soungarth. Say hello to everyone at Ridmouth. Say hello to everyone at Faimbury. Say hello to everyone at Dyndham. Say hello to everyone at Sealmouth.

— Winifred Vanridge
