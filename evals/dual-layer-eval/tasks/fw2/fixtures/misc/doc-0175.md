# Postcard from Skye

We saw seals. Back on shift Monday. Weather was steady drizzle most days. Say hello to everyone at Thorrcote. Weather was low cloud that never lifted most days. Back on shift on the fourteenth. Weather was bright sun and a cold wind most days. Back on shift after the bank holiday. Say hello to everyone at Gaincombe. Weather was a mild, grey day most days. The kids loved it. The kids made friends with a goat. We got rained off twice. We found a pub with a piano. We walked further than planned. Say hello to everyone at Wourrfield. Weather was sleet showers most days. Say hello to everyone at Soungarth. Say hello to everyone at Grondby. Say hello to everyone at Colbury. Say hello to everyone at Mounbury. We ate far too much. Say hello to everyone at Lorrcote. Back on shift next week. Say hello to everyone at Ridmouth. Say hello to everyone at Gydham. Say hello to everyone at Dyndham. Say hello to everyone at Stearrholm. Weather was the first real heat of the year most days. Say hello to everyone at Graidfield. The kids wants to move there. My mother wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Taidhope. My mother made friends with a goat. The dog complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Starrmouth. Say hello to everyone at Drorcote. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. Say hello to everyone at Sealmouth. Say hello to everyone at Tombury. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. Say hello to everyone at Braisbury. The kids complained throughout. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. Say hello to everyone at Thinmouth. Say hello to everyone at Welbury. Our neighbour complained throughout. The dog made friends with a goat. Say hello to everyone at Tiscote.

— Clemency Bainlow
