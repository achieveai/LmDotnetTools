# Postcard from a campsite near Bruges

Say hello to everyone at Thourstead. Back on shift on the fourteenth. Say hello to everyone at Mounbury. The dog loved it. Say hello to everyone at Lorrcote. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. The dog made friends with a goat. We walked further than planned. Weather was steady drizzle most days. The dog wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Tombury. We lost the car keys once. My mother complained throughout. The kids wants to move there. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. We ate far too much. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. The kids loved it. Say hello to everyone at Drorcote. We found a pub with a piano. Say hello to everyone at Gaincombe. My mother made friends with a goat. We saw seals. Back on shift after the bank holiday. Say hello to everyone at Dyndham. Back on shift next week. The dog complained throughout. Weather was a hard frost most days. Say hello to everyone at Dralbury. Our neighbour made friends with a goat. Say hello to everyone at Stearrholm. Our neighbour loved it. Back on shift Monday. My mother wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Colbury. Our neighbour wants to move there. We got rained off twice. Say hello to everyone at Coscombe. Say hello to everyone at Gydham. Weather was low cloud that never lifted most days. Say hello to everyone at Wourrfield. Say hello to everyone at Daidhope.

— Katya Kurley
