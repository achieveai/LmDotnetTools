# Postcard from a cousin's wedding in Cork

Back on shift on the fourteenth. The dog wants to move there. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. Say hello to everyone at Tusholm. Back on shift Monday. We got rained off twice. Say hello to everyone at Gydham. Say hello to everyone at Tombury. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. Say hello to everyone at Welbury. Back on shift after the bank holiday. The kids complained throughout. We lost the car keys once. Say hello to everyone at Thyrrholm. Say hello to everyone at Colbury. Weather was sleet showers most days. Back on shift next week. We ate far too much. Say hello to everyone at Thummouth. Weather was a hard frost most days. My mother complained throughout. Weather was steady drizzle most days. We found a pub with a piano. We walked further than planned. The dog complained throughout. We saw seals. My mother wants to move there. Our neighbour made friends with a goat. Weather was a mild, grey day most days. Weather was the first real heat of the year most days. Say hello to everyone at Thinmouth. Say hello to everyone at Drorcote. Say hello to everyone at Taidhope. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. Say hello to everyone at Lorrcote. Say hello to everyone at Teasham. The kids wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Ridmouth. The dog loved it. Our neighbour loved it. My mother loved it. Say hello to everyone at Faimbury. The kids loved it. Say hello to everyone at Suscombe. Our neighbour wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Thorrcote. Say hello to everyone at Stearrholm.

— Ulric Vasmore
