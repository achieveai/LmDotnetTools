# Postcard from the Outer Hebrides

Say hello to everyone at Soungarth. Back on shift next week. Weather was low cloud that never lifted most days. Say hello to everyone at Thyrrholm. We walked further than planned. Weather was the first real heat of the year most days. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. We found a pub with a piano. The dog made friends with a goat. Our neighbour made friends with a goat. My mother complained throughout. Weather was a hard frost most days. Back on shift after the bank holiday. The kids loved it. Say hello to everyone at Ridmouth. Say hello to everyone at Welbury. Back on shift Monday. The kids made friends with a goat. Our neighbour complained throughout. Say hello to everyone at Daidhope. We saw seals. Back on shift on the fourteenth. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. Say hello to everyone at Thinmouth. We got rained off twice. Weather was a mild, grey day most days. Weather was bright sun and a cold wind most days. We lost the car keys once. Say hello to everyone at Taidhope. My mother loved it. Say hello to everyone at Suscombe. Our neighbour loved it. Say hello to everyone at Gydham. Weather was steady drizzle most days. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. Say hello to everyone at Fymby. The dog loved it. Say hello to everyone at Drorcote. We ate far too much. Weather was sleet showers most days. Say hello to everyone at Tusholm. Say hello to everyone at Dyndham. Our neighbour wants to move there. My mother wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Dralbury.

— Perpetua Groulard
