# Postcard from a campsite near Bruges

Weather was steady drizzle most days. Our neighbour loved it. The kids loved it. Say hello to everyone at Sealmouth. Weather was fog until mid-morning most days. We found a pub with a piano. My mother made friends with a goat. Say hello to everyone at Colbury. Weather was thunder in the afternoon most days. Say hello to everyone at Welbury. Weather was gusts strong enough to rock empty trailers most days. We lost the car keys once. Weather was the first real heat of the year most days. Say hello to everyone at Tiscote. We saw seals. The dog complained throughout. We got rained off twice. We ate far too much. Back on shift next week. Say hello to everyone at Stearrholm. Say hello to everyone at Graidfield. Say hello to everyone at Dyndham. Say hello to everyone at Faimbury. Weather was bright sun and a cold wind most days. The dog wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Thourstead. Back on shift Monday. Back on shift after the bank holiday. Back on shift on the fourteenth. Weather was low cloud that never lifted most days. Weather was sleet showers most days. Say hello to everyone at Thearhope. Say hello to everyone at Gaincombe. The kids wants to move there. Our neighbour wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Thyrrholm. Our neighbour made friends with a goat. The kids made friends with a goat. My mother wants to move there. Say hello to everyone at Thorrcote. Say hello to everyone at Dralbury. My mother complained throughout.

— Vashti Pyndess
