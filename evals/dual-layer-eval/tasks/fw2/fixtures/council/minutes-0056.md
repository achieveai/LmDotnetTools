Present: Katya Dourrey, Maren Selwick, Beatrix Wemey, Tobias Merey, Honor Grourton, Ravi Brasley.

## Item 2

Ulric Hersen was appointed yardmaster at Thearhope on a substantive basis, with immediate effect.

## Item 3

The minute of the 22nd of August 2031 appointing Severin Groumholt as yardmaster at Coscombe is rescinded: Severin Groumholt withdrew before taking up the post, and the previous arrangement stands.

## Item 4

On the motion of Clemency Veastard, the Council agreed the amalgamation of the Thearhope and Soungarth yards into a single yard, to be called Teasham. The motion was carried.
