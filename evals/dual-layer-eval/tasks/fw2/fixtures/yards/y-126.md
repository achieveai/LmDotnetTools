# Around our yards: Thummouth

Before the carriers came, Thummouth was known for a tannery that nobody misses. The name is said to come from nobody quite knows where. Before the carriers came, Thummouth was known for its annual horse fair. The name is said to come from an old word for a ford. Parish records from the 1790s mention three wheelwrights. Local people still hold a fete on the green each July. A coaching inn stood where the weighbridge is now. A water mill stood where the weighbridge is now.
