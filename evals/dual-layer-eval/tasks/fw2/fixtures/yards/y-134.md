# Around our yards: Hallam Cross

The name is said to come from an old word for a ford. Local people still point out the one cottage that stayed dry in 1953. Local people still call the lane by its old name. Before the carriers came, Hallam Cross was known for a tannery that nobody misses. Gisela Reandmore lent us her grandfather’s ledger for this piece.
