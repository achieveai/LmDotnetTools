## Kofi Brandt (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 17 Mar 2026 07:51 UTC

Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. There was no platform incident at that time; other customers in the same region were unaffected.

The health-check path was changed before the new route existed, so every instance was marked unhealthy and removed. Once it was reverted, the site came straight back. Our external monitoring recorded `ravham-v2.tamberlow.app` as unavailable from 17:34 UTC on Sunday 15 March 2026 until 20:46 UTC. Our knowledge base has a checklist for changes like this one; the order of the steps matters. Let us know if you see anything like it again.

Kofi Brandt
