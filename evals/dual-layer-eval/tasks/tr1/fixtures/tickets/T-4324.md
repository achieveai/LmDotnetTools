## Dev Rathmore (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 10 Feb 2026 18:37 UTC

We've finished the investigation. There was no maintenance planned for that time, so this was not scheduled work.

This took a while to pin down. After a certificate rotation on our side, the health checks in our load-balancer pool for your region marked every backend as failed, so traffic had nowhere to go. Monitoring shows `corrley.tamberlow.app` fully down for 139 minutes, starting at 07:21 UTC on Monday 26 January 2026. I'd expect this to be covered, but the review panel makes the call. The fix has been rolled out to every region, and we are reviewing why our tests did not catch it. Thanks for bearing with us.

Dev Rathmore
