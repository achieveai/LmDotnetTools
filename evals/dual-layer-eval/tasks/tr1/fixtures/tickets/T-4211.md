## Priya Ashcombe (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 24 Jan 2026 09:49 UTC

Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. This took a while to pin down. Your login goes through Keyholt SSO, the identity provider your company contracts for itself. Keyholt SSO was unavailable, so nobody could sign in; our side answered every request it received.

Our external monitoring recorded `quenham-app.tamberlow.app` as unavailable from 19:49 UTC on Thursday 22 January 2026 until 21:11 UTC. If you'd like, we can review any similar change with you in a ticket before you make it. We'll keep the ticket open for a couple of days in case anything else comes up.
