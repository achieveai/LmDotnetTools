## Oskar Lind (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 10 Mar 2026 01:01 UTC

Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. I know your developer suspected your own DNS; that was a reasonable guess, but the records were fine.

In short: a deployment we made to our routing layer that afternoon carried a wrong connection-pool setting, so new connections were refused until we rolled it back. Monitoring shows `fellshaw-app.tamberlow.app` fully down for 103 minutes, starting at 19:24 UTC on Monday 16 February 2026. We are also changing our runbook so that a failover like this one is escalated after 3 minutes rather than waiting. We'll keep the ticket open for a couple of days in case anything else comes up.
