Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

A disk controller failed in the host your database runs on, and failover took far longer than it should have because of a bug in our orchestration, which we have since patched. Per our uptime checks, `pellshaw-web.tamberlow.app` was unreachable on Tuesday 27 January 2026 from 20:42 UTC until 23:08 UTC. We've added an alert on the specific condition that caused this, so we should catch it earlier next time. Let us know if you see anything like it again.
