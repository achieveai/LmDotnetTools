## Priya Ashcombe (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 09 Mar 2026 10:51 UTC

We've finished the investigation. In short: your domain's DNS is hosted with Dunmore Domains, the registrar you chose when you bought it, not with us. Dunmore Domains had a fault and your records stopped resolving; the site itself was up the whole time.

Our external monitoring recorded `ravthorpe-app.tamberlow.app` as unavailable from 08:32 UTC on Saturday 7 March 2026 until 10:56 UTC. A second pair of eyes on changes like this usually helps; your agency could be a good choice. Sorry again for the disruption.

Priya Ashcombe
