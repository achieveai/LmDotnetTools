## Carys Pennick (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 23 Mar 2026 17:35 UTC

We've finished the investigation. There was no maintenance planned for that time, so this was not scheduled work.

What happened: our database proxy tier ran out of memory after a configuration change we pushed, and restarted in a loop until we reverted it. Per our uptime checks, `yarrford-agency.tamberlow.app` was unreachable on Saturday 21 March 2026 from 17:20 UTC until 21:18 UTC. We've added an alert on the specific condition that caused this, so we should catch it earlier next time. We'll keep the ticket open for a couple of days in case anything else comes up.

Carys Pennick
