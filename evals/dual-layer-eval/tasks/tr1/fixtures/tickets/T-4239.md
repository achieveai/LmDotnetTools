## Kofi Brandt (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 27 Jan 2026 04:14 UTC

Thanks for your patience while we investigated. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

In short: a network change in our primary region withdrew a route by mistake, and our edge could no longer reach the application servers. Monitoring shows `fellshaw-web.tamberlow.app` fully down for 28 minutes, starting at 03:49 UTC on Monday 26 January 2026. We are also changing our runbook so that a failover like this one is escalated after 10 minutes rather than waiting. Let us know if you see anything like it again.
