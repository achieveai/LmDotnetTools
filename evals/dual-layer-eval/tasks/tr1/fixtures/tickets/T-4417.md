## Kofi Brandt (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 01 Mar 2026 20:09 UTC

We've finished the investigation. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

We found that after a certificate rotation on our side, the health checks in our load-balancer pool for your region marked every backend as failed, so traffic had nowhere to go. Monitoring shows `yarrcombe-web.tamberlow.app` fully down for 20 minutes, starting at 20:38 UTC on Friday 27 February 2026. We've added an alert on the specific condition that caused this, so we should catch it earlier next time. Sorry again for the disruption.

Kofi Brandt
