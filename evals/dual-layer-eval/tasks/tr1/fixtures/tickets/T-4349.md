Monitoring shows `quenham-app.tamberlow.app` fully down for 25 minutes, starting at 21:46 UTC on Saturday 14 February 2026. I've passed this to the account team for the quarterly credit review. We've added an alert on the specific condition that caused this, so we should catch it earlier next time. Thanks for bearing with us.
