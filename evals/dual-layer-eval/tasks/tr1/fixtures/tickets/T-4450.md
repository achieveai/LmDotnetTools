## Hanne Solberg (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 06 Mar 2026 14:39 UTC

Thanks for your patience while we investigated. Here is the short version. A rate-limit rule applied to every path, including the ones our edge uses, throttled all traffic to nothing. Once it was reverted, the site came straight back.

Probe data for `ravham-v2.tamberlow.app`: first failed check at 12:00 UTC on Wednesday 4 March 2026, first successful check at 14:36 UTC. Our knowledge base has a checklist for changes like this one; the order of the steps matters. Thanks for bearing with us.
