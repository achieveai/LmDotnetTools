## Priya Ashcombe (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 04 Feb 2026 09:10 UTC

We've finished the investigation. What happened: Veltrane Edge, the content-delivery partner that sits in front of all sites we host, had edge nodes in your region dropping connections; our own servers were healthy and waiting.

Monitoring shows `yarrley-web.tamberlow.app` fully down for 132 minutes, starting at 11:19 UTC on Monday 2 February 2026. We've added an alert on the specific condition that caused this, so we should catch it earlier next time. Sorry again for the disruption.
