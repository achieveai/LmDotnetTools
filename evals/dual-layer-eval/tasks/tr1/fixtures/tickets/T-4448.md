## Priya Ashcombe (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 06 Mar 2026 07:18 UTC

Apologies for the wait. Our findings are below. For completeness, Veltrane Edge, the content-delivery partner in front of hosted sites, was healthy throughout.

In short: after a certificate rotation on our side, the health checks in our load-balancer pool for your region marked every backend as failed, so traffic had nowhere to go. Probe data for `varnby-web.tamberlow.app`: first failed check at 23:28 UTC on Wednesday 4 March 2026, first successful check at 00:44 UTC on Thursday 5 March 2026. Some of that time overlaps planned work, which is shown on our status page. We are also changing our runbook so that a failover like this one is escalated after 11 minutes rather than waiting. Sorry again for the disruption.
