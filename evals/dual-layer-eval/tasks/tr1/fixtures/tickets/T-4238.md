## Carys Pennick (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 27 Jan 2026 09:56 UTC

Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

What happened: a disk controller failed in the host your database runs on, and failover took far longer than it should have because of a bug in our orchestration, which we have since patched. Per our uptime checks, `yarrford.tamberlow.app` was unreachable on Monday 26 January 2026 from 10:56 UTC until 11:29 UTC. We are also changing our runbook so that a failover like this one is escalated after 11 minutes rather than waiting. Let us know if you see anything like it again.

Carys Pennick
