## Carys Pennick (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 08 Mar 2026 06:03 UTC

Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. This took a while to pin down. A deployment we made to our routing layer that afternoon carried a wrong connection-pool setting, so new connections were refused until we rolled it back.

Per our uptime checks, `pellshaw-app.tamberlow.app` was unreachable on Thursday 5 March 2026 from 19:13 UTC until 21:31 UTC. We've added an alert on the specific condition that caused this, so we should catch it earlier next time. Thanks for bearing with us.

Carys Pennick
