## Priya Ashcombe (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 19 Mar 2026 07:39 UTC

We've finished the investigation. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

Here is the short version. A deployment we made to our routing layer that afternoon carried a wrong connection-pool setting, so new connections were refused until we rolled it back. Our external monitoring recorded `ordcombe-v2.tamberlow.app` as unavailable from 14:47 UTC on Tuesday 17 March 2026 until 15:02 UTC. The fix has been rolled out to every region, and we are reviewing why our tests did not catch it. Thanks for bearing with us.

Priya Ashcombe
