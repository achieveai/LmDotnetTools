## Dev Rathmore (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 04 Mar 2026 04:42 UTC

We've finished the investigation. Having gone through the logs and our change history, a certificate was uploaded without its intermediate chain and browsers refused to connect. Once it was reverted, the site came straight back.

Our external monitoring recorded `yarrley-v2.tamberlow.app` as unavailable from 20:33 UTC on Sunday 1 March 2026 until 22:47 UTC. Our credit review panel will look at this with the rest of the quarter's tickets. Our knowledge base has a checklist for changes like this one; the order of the steps matters.
