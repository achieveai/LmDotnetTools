## Hanne Solberg (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 24 Jan 2026 11:57 UTC

Apologies for the wait. Our findings are below. I appreciate it looked like one of our outages from where you sat, but our platform was healthy.

Having gone through the logs and our change history, the database password was rotated but the application secret was not updated, so every request failed to connect. Once it was reverted, the site came straight back. Probe data for `ordcombe-v2.tamberlow.app`: first failed check at 06:31 UTC on Friday 23 January 2026, first successful check at 07:29 UTC. For next time, it's worth keeping a note of changes made to the account, with times, so they are easy to match to problems. Let us know if you see anything like it again.

Hanne Solberg
