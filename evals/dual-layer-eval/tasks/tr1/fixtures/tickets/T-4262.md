## Oskar Lind (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 03 Feb 2026 06:50 UTC

Thank you for the logs; here's what we found. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

Having gone through the logs and our change history, a network change in our primary region withdrew a route by mistake, and our edge could no longer reach the application servers. Per our uptime checks, `yarrford-web.tamberlow.app` was unreachable on Sunday 1 February 2026 from 10:59 UTC until 12:48 UTC. A written incident review will go to affected customers within 16 working days. We'll keep the ticket open for a couple of days in case anything else comes up.
