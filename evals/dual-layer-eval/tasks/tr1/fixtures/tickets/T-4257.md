## Hanne Solberg (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 02 Feb 2026 00:47 UTC

Apologies for the wait. Our findings are below. We first checked whether anything on your side had changed: your firewall rules, DNS records and deploy history are all untouched, so this was not you.

Having gone through the logs and our change history, a deployment we made to our routing layer that afternoon carried a wrong connection-pool setting, so new connections were refused until we rolled it back. Probe data for `tullden-v2.tamberlow.app`: first failed check at 14:32 UTC on Friday 30 January 2026, first successful check at 14:50 UTC. The fix has been rolled out to every region, and we are reviewing why our tests did not catch it. We'll keep the ticket open for a couple of days in case anything else comes up.
