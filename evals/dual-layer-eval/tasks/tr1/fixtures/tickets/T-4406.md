## Tomas Vell (Tamberlow Hosting Support) — 26 Feb 2026 10:34 UTC

Apologies for the wait. Our findings are below. I know your developer suspected your own DNS; that was a reasonable guess, but the records were fine.

In short: our database proxy tier ran out of memory after a configuration change we pushed, and restarted in a loop until we reverted it. Our external monitoring recorded `yarrley-web.tamberlow.app` as unavailable from 20:41 UTC on Tuesday 24 February 2026 until 22:19 UTC. The fix has been rolled out to every region, and we are reviewing why our tests did not catch it. Thanks for bearing with us.

Tomas Vell
