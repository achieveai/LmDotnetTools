# Raving Rabbids: Alive & Kicking

Raving Rabbids: Alive & Kicking, known as "Rabbids: Fuori di schermo" in Italy, is a party video game developed by Ubisoft Milan and published on November 3, 2011 in Australia, November 4, 2011 in Europe and November 8, 2011 in North America for the Xbox 360. This is the sixth game from the "Raving Rabbids" games franchise and it is the first Rabbids game not released on a Nintendo platform. The game was exclusively developed for the Kinect and consists of mini-games with up to 4 players.
