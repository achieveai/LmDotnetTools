# Tears of the Sun

Willis produced "Tears of the Sun" through Cheyenne Enterprises, his production company, and took the title from an early sub–title for "Live Free or Die Hard", the fourth film in the "Die Hard" series. He filmed the sequel on the condition that he could use its sub-title for his SEALs war film. The cast of "Tears of the Sun" includes actual African refugees living in the United States.
