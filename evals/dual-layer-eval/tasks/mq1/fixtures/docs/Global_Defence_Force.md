# Global Defence Force

Global Defence Force, known in Japan as is a PlayStation 2 third-person shooter video game developed by Sandlot. The game was originally published as "The Chikyū Bōeigun 2" in Japan by D3 Publisher, as volume 81 of the "Simple 2000 series" of budget games. It was later released in Europe by Essential Games, the brand created by D3 Publisher to publish Simple 2000 series games in Europe.
