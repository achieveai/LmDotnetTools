# Lebedyansky District

Lebedyansky District () is an administrative and municipal district (raion), one of the eighteen in Lipetsk Oblast, Russia. It is located in the northern central part of the oblast. The area of the district is . Its administrative center is the town of Lebedyan. Population: 46,644 (2002 Census); The population of Lebedyan accounts for 48.7% of the district's total population.
