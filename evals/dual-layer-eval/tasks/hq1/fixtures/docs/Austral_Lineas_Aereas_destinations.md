# Austral Líneas Aéreas destinations

Following is a list of destinations currently served by Austral Líneas Aéreas, as of 2013 . Each destination is provided with the country name, the name of the airport served, and both its International Air Transport Association (IATA) three-letter designator (IATA airport code) and its International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) four-letter designator (ICAO airport code). The list also includes the airports that serve either as a hub or as a focus city for the carrier. Terminated destinations are additionally presented.
