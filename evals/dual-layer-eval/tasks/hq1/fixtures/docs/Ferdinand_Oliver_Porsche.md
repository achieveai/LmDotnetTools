# Ferdinand Oliver Porsche

Ferdinand Oliver Porsche (born 13 March 1961 in Stuttgart) is a German lawyer and member of the Supervisory Board of the German sports car manufacturer Porsche. Son of Ferdinand Alexander Porsche and great-grandson of automotive pioneer Ferdinand Porsche, he completed his M. A. studies in Law, Publishing, and Political Science at the University of Salzburg, subsequently taking his Doctor’s Degree in Law and his MBA at the University of Miami.
