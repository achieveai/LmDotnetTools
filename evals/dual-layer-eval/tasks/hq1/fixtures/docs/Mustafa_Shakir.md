# Mustafa Shakir

Before acting, Shakir was a barber. He grew up in Harlem and attended a local theater reading where he was picked up. Afterwards, he was hooked up with various acting groups. He attended The New School and graduated in 2001. The actors that Shakir most admires are Viola Davis and Morgan Freeman. Shakir is also an avid comic book fan and has expressed interest in playing Blade or Spawn. He has instead been cast as John McIver, Bushemaster, in the second season of "Luke Cage".
