# Dover Street Market

Dover Street Market is a high fashion company and multi-brand retailer originally located on Dover Street, in Mayfair, London. It has stores in New York City, Tokyo, and Singapore. Dover Street Market was created by Rei Kawakubo of Japanese fashion label Comme des Garçons and her husband Adrian Joffe, and sells Comme des Garçons and complementary high fashion brands such as Ann Demeulemeester, Balenciaga, Céline, Gucci, Hussein Chalayan, Junya Watanabe, J.W.Anderson, Lanvin, LOEWE, Maison Margiela, Marni,
