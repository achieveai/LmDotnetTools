# Julian Bickersteth

Kenneth Julian Faithfull Bickersteth, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (5 July 1885 – 16 October 1962) was a British Anglican priest, chaplain, and headmaster. He started his career as a school chaplain and teacher. During World War I, he served as a military chaplain. Between the wars he was headmaster of The Collegiate School of St Peter in Adelaide, Australia, and then headmaster of Felsted School in Essex, England. From 1942 to 1958, he served as Archdeacon of Maidstone in the Diocese of Canterbury, Church of England.
