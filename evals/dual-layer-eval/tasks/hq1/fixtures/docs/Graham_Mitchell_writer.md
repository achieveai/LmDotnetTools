# Graham Mitchell (writer)

Graham Mitchell is a television scriptwriter. He has written 27 episodes of "The Bill", as lead writer including the 2005 live episode. He has also written for the Casualty, Mersey Beat, Holby Blue, "Holby City", and The Body Farm (TV series). He has been part of the regular core writing team on awarding-winning Holby City since 2006, writing 19 episodes to date.
