# Wendi Deng Murdoch

Wendi Deng Murdoch (; born December 8, 1968) is a Chinese-American actress, producer, and businesswoman. She was the third wife of News Corporation chairman and CEO Rupert Murdoch, who filed for divorce from her in June 2013. Deng's debut in the media came with Fox TV, and she was subsequently offered an internship at Star TV in Hong Kong, part of News Corporation.
