# Tom Goode (politician)

Thomas Henry "Tom" Goode (November 9, 1900 – September 12, 1983) was a Liberal party member of the Canadian House of Commons, representing the district of Burnaby—Richmond from 1949 to 1957. His son, Thomas Henry Goode, later represented the same electoral district from 1968 to 1972. Tom Goode died in Surrey in 1983 of a heart attack.
