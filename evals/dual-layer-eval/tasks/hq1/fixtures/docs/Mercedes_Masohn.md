# Mercedes Masohn

Mercedes Mason ( ; born Masöhn), Born 1983) is a Swedish actress known for playing the role of Zondra in the American television series "Chuck" and the role of Isabel Zambada in the American procedural drama "The Finder". She starred in the 2011 American horror film "". She played Louise Leonard in the 2012–13 American supernatural drama "666 Park Avenue" and Talia Del Campo in "". She is currently a regular on AMC's television series "Fear the Walking Dead".
